# Decision 0002: proceed with Plan A

**Date:** 2026-09-21  
**Status:** Phase 1 recommendation; awaits human gate review

Proceed with the full multi-dataset skill/performance design because three
datasets provide eligible, complementary trial-level targets with stable
participant IDs:

1. JIGSAWS expert-rated GRS (103 trials, 8 participants).
2. BGU-PatCut scan-derived total error, TE (1,254 documented usable trials,
   54 participants).
3. ROSMA independently observed task-error count (206 matched kinematic/target
   trials, 12 participants).

BGU-SKILLS contributes identity recoverability and longitudinal/shift
robustness only. Its published performance metrics—completion time, path length,
and orientation-change rate—are derived from the kinematic input, and the
deposited tissue scans do not include a validated accuracy label.

The publication must use “skill/performance” language: only JIGSAWS supplies an
expert-rated skill construct. PatCut TE and ROSMA Errors measure task-specific
objective performance.
