I am preparing a manuscript titled “Operator identity and out-of-user generalization in automated surgical skill assessment: a multi-dataset evaluation,” using existing JIGSAWS, ROSMA, BGU-SKILLS, and BGU-PatCut surgical-training datasets. Analysis has already been conducted; I am seeking guidance before journal submission and do not wish to imply that prior Cornell approval was obtained.

The analysis code uses kinematics, performance outcomes, and coded participant labels. There was no new participant recruitment or interaction in this project. One analysis trains classifiers to predict dataset participant codes across repeated trials; its purpose is to assess operator-specific information and model validation, rather than to identify participants by name. JIGSAWS is subject to a research access agreement, while the other datasets were obtained from research repositories.

Could you advise whether this project constitutes human-participant research, whether it qualifies for a not-human-participant-research determination, or whether another review process is required? Please also advise what documentation is needed given that analysis has already taken place, and what ethics wording would be appropriate for publication.

I can provide the protocol, manuscript, source dataset documentation and access terms, and a description of identifiers, keys, media, and metadata accessible to the research team. Please let me know how best to provide those materials securely and whether a RASS-IRB submission is needed.
