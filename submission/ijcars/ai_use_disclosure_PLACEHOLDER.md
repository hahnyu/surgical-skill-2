# AI-use disclosure — author must complete

The journal requires the human authors to disclose substantive use of generative AI. Replace the placeholder in `ai_use_disclosure.md` before submission with a statement written and approved by the authors.

Address these points in your own words:

- Name the AI tool and provider.
- State the tasks it assisted with, such as code drafting, analysis implementation, literature discovery, manuscript structuring, or language editing.
- Explain that the authors independently checked the underlying data, executed and validated the analyses, verified citations and numerical results, and revised the text.
- State that the AI tool is not an author and that the human authors accept full responsibility for the work.

The build inserts `ai_use_disclosure.md` in Methods. Rebuild after editing it; do not edit only generated LaTeX. Do not submit the placeholder. Describe only verification the authors actually performed.
