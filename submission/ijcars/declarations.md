# Statements and declarations

## Ethics approval and consent

This study was a secondary analysis of previously collected surgical-training datasets. All authors confirmed the investigators' authorized access to the study data and compliance with the applicable data-use permissions and consent restrictions. No investigator could identify individual participants or access a re-identification key. No participants were recruited or contacted, and no new participant interventions or data collection were undertaken. Analyses used kinematic records, performance outcomes, and coded participant labels; identity classification concerned dataset codes rather than the identification of individuals. No additional informed consent was obtained for this secondary analysis. Based on these data-access circumstances, the authors considered the project not to involve identifiable human participants. No formal ethics determination was obtained from Cornell University; this statement does not represent an institutional approval or exemption.

## Data and code availability

Analysis code, configurations, aggregate results, and the corrected submission sources are publicly available at \url{https://github.com/hahnyu/surgical-skill-2/tree/codex/ijcars-submission}. The dated correction is documented in `results/corrections/2026-11-16/`, with input/output hashes in its analysis manifests and submission-source/PDF hashes in `submission/ijcars/package_manifest.json`. Original locked artifacts are retained for provenance and are distinguished from the corrected outputs. JIGSAWS is available from its custodians under their access terms and is not redistributed. ROSMA, BGU-SKILLS, and BGU-PatCut are available from the cited source repositories. Raw data and participant-level prediction files are not included in the code repository; reproduction of analyses requiring those files requires obtaining the source data under the applicable terms.
