# Optional Cornell IRB confirmation request

Cornell’s guidance states that analysis of existing de-identified data may be considered not human-participant research when investigators have no identifiers or re-identification key. The manuscript uses that guidance and does not claim an IRB protocol number.

If IJCARS requests an institutional determination letter, send the following to `irbhp@cornell.edu` or request a consultation through Cornell Research Services.

**Subject:** Request for confirmation of not-human-participant-research status for secondary dataset analysis

Dear Cornell IRB team,

I am requesting confirmation of the appropriate determination for a manuscript titled “Operator identity and out-of-user generalization in automated surgical skill assessment: a multi-dataset evaluation.”

The project is a secondary analysis of four previously collected surgical-training datasets: JIGSAWS, ROSMA, BGU-SKILLS, and BGU-PatCut. The research team had no interaction or intervention with participants. We received or downloaded only coded/de-identified records, had no direct identifiers, had no access to any key linking codes to identities, and did not attempt re-identification. Results are reported in aggregate. The source investigators are not involved as data providers with access to a linkage key for this analysis.

Could you please confirm whether this activity is considered not human-participant research under Cornell policy and whether any RASS-IRB submission or formal determination letter is required for journal publication? I can provide the protocol, data-access terms, data dictionary, and manuscript if helpful.

Sincerely,
