# Claim-to-evidence matrix

This matrix is the language gate for the manuscript. “Allowed wording” is the strongest wording supported by the locked analysis. Broader language requires new evidence.

| ID | Manuscript claim | Design/estimand | Locked evidence | Allowed wording | Prohibited extrapolation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Same-participant evaluation can be more favorable than held-out-participant evaluation | Paired protocol gap for identical features/model | `phase3_protocol_gap.csv`; Figure 2; positive standardized gap in 6/7 tasks; JIGSAWS skill gaps +0.400 to +0.479 with intervals above zero | “Protocol performance differed; same-participant was more favorable in most tasks.” | “All published skill models are inflated.” |
| C2 | Operator identity is recoverable from unsuppressed summary kinematics | Nested linear/RF probes versus record-wise identity permutations | `phase3_identity_probes.csv`, `phase3_identity_null_distribution.csv`; 10/10 groups above null, each *p*=0.0196 | “Identity-compatible information was recoverable by tested probes.” | “The representation contains a unique biometric signature” or “all identity is known.” |
| C3 | JIGSAWS same-participant skill classification permits identity-compatible lookup | Participant-wise versus record-wise label permutation | `phase5_permutation_outputs.csv`; medians differ +0.444 to +0.538, each *p*=0.004975 | “Person-compatible structure can sustain same-participant classification when labels are constant within person.” | “The observed classifier used only identity.” |
| C4 | I3 reduces tested identity recovery | Paired I3−I0 fresh-probe balanced accuracy | `phase5_effect_summaries.csv`; linear −0.184 [−0.215,−0.151], nonlinear −0.138 [−0.175,−0.098] | “I3 reduced fresh linear and nonlinear probe recovery.” | “I3 removed all identity information.” |
| C5 | I4 was not verified on both probes | I4−capacity-control probe differences | `phase5_effect_summaries.csv`; linear −0.048 [−0.082,−0.015], nonlinear −0.003 [−0.038,+0.032] | “I4 failed the prespecified two-probe verification rule.” | “Adversarial learning cannot suppress identity.” |
| C6 | I3 did not consistently improve new-user utility | Participant-average held-out I3−I0 paired MAE | `phase5_participant_task_effects.csv`; Figure 5; 1/7 point estimates favorable, 6/7 unfavorable; six intervals cross zero | “Verified suppression did not consistently improve held-out-participant prediction.” | “Identity suppression never works” or “I3 definitively harms all tasks.” |
| C7 | Most task effects remain imprecise | 2,000 participant bootstrap replicates | `phase5_bootstrap_replicates.parquet`; task-specific intervals | “Compatible ranges include benefit and harm for most tasks; equivalence was not tested.” | “There was no effect.” |
| C8 | Random dimension removal does not explain I3 probe changes | I3-matched random orthogonal projection | `phase4_ablation_log.csv`; random projection worse than I3 by +0.167 linear and +0.149 nonlinear | “Targeted learned directions, rather than dimension loss alone, explain the measured probe reduction.” | “The exact biological identity mechanism was isolated.” |
| C9 | Duration alone does not explain identity recovery | Duration probe and I1/I2 controls | `phase3_identity_probes.csv`, `phase4_ablation_log.csv` | “Duration-only and duration-control results did not account for full identity recovery.” | “Duration is unrelated to skill or identity.” |
| C10 | I3 did not consistently reduce same-participant person-level skill accuracy | Participant-average JIGSAWS I3−I0 accuracy | `phase5_participant_skill_effects.csv`; −0.025, 0.000, +0.025 with intervals | “The prespecified consistent-reduction hypothesis was not supported.” | “Identity is irrelevant to same-participant classification.” |
| C11 | External outcomes are objective task-performance measures, not expert ratings | Source provenance audit | `TARGET_PROVENANCE.md`, Table 1 | “ROSMA errors” and “PatCut image-derived cutting error.” | “Expert skill scores” for ROSMA, PatCut, or BGU-SKILLS. |
| C12 | JIGSAWS overlap is replication/extension, not independent validation | Same 103 trials as Paper 1; new features/interventions/external datasets | `phase3_paper1_replication.csv`; `0001-paper1-provenance.md` | “JIGSAWS replication and mechanistic extension with external datasets.” | “Independent JIGSAWS validation.” |
| C13 | Clinical deployment validity is unknown | No clinical cohort or prospective test | Study design and limitations | “Dry-lab findings require prospective clinical validation.” | Claims of competence, certification, patient benefit, or safety. |
| C14 | Fairness was not evaluated | Demographic variables unavailable | TRIPOD+AI checklist item 14 | “Demographic performance disparities could not be assessed.” | “The model is fair” or equating participant identity suppression with demographic fairness. |
